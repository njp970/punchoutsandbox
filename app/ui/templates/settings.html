What we will not POST to, and why

https only, port 443 Your shared secret travels in the document body. Plain HTTP would publish it to every hop on the path.
Public addresses only Every address your hostname resolves to must be globally routable — not loopback, private or link-local.
The address is pinned We connect to the exact address we checked, while still presenting your hostname for TLS. Checking DNS and then connecting by name leaves a rebinding window.
No redirects A 302 to an internal address would walk past every check above. A redirect is reported to you as the response it is.
Valid certificate Self-signed certificates are refused. A sandbox that skipped verification would teach the wrong lesson about a channel carrying secrets.

This is not paranoia about your endpoint. "Take a URL from a stranger and POST to it" is the textbook definition of server-side request forgery, and this sandbox runs inside someone else's AWS account.
