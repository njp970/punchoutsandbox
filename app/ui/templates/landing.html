A supplier to test your punchout against

Free, hosted, and running right now. Point your buyer system at it and find out what your documents actually look like to the other side.

The problem this solves

Every free punchout tool runs the wrong way round. They simulate a buyer, so a supplier can check their catalogue works. If you are building the buyer side, there is nothing to point at — so you end up testing your integration against itself.

Round-tripping your own build and parse proves the two halves agree with each other. It does not prove either one conforms. If both share a misreading, every test passes and the first real supplier rejects every document.

What is missing is an independent judge. This is one.
