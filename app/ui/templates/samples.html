Why these are generated rather than written

A sample typed into a page is correct on the day it is typed and silently wrong after the next change to the code that produces the real document. A wrong example in the documentation of a conformance tool is worse than no example at all, because people trust it. These come from the builders themselves, and the test suite asserts every one of them validates against the real DTD.
